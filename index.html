01 Robotics
Nov 2026
Completed

BeltBot

A pick-and-place cell: a conveyor belt carries parts, a camera detects them and stops the belt, and a myCobot 280 arm picks each part and drops it in the right box by shape.

  • Arduino belt control (speed, direction, start/stop) driven from Python.
  • OpenCV vision detection and hand-taught robot positions.
  • Digital twin: an interface to switch between the real robot and a virtual one (simulated myCobot, belt, camera and box) and save positions.
  • Object-oriented Python, written and documented in English.
